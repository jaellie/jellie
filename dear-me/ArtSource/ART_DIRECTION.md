# Dear Me, — Art direction

Keep these rules for every scene, sprite and UI illustration.

1. **Always gradients, always rich color.** No flat fills. Every wash, sky, wall and floor is a
   gradient (warm ↔ cool), saturated enough to glow on cream paper.
2. **The sky matters most.** Whenever a sky is visible (window, street, rooftop), give it the
   richest color in the scene: layered gradients (e.g. indigo → violet → rose → amber → gold at the
   horizon), clouds lit from below, light breaking through.
3. **Warm light.** Light sources (lamp, sunset, café windows, city lights) bloom softly and spill
   warm color onto nearby surfaces. Even night and rain scenes keep a warm light somewhere.
4. **Style mix.** Urban Sketch line (wobbly sepia fineliner, overshoot, gaps) + Watercolor wash
   (cream cold-press paper, uneven pigment, dried edges, granulation) + Paper Lantern glow at night.
   Paintings are full-bleed: color reaches every edge of the 9:16 frame (no paper border).
5. **Portrait 9:16 (1080×1920).** Keep important things inside x 0.1–0.9 (tall phones crop the
   sides) and above y 0.75 (the dialogue panel covers the bottom).
6. Day scenes are bright and safe; night scenes are reflective but never murky.

Tools: `painter-lib.js` (`linear`, `radial`, `wash`, `paint`, `glow`, `ink`, `reserve`).
Render with `node ArtSource/render.js`.

## Characters

Yuri, Yunji and Hyejin are slender, delicate college girls (여리여리): small oval faces with
smiling closed eyes, a small mouth and soft cheek blush; narrow sloped shoulders and thin arms;
pastel tops. Yuri — long straight brown hair, coral cardigan. Yunji — black bob, sage blouse.
Hyejin — chestnut ponytail with a pink ribbon, lavender top, always holding her camera.
