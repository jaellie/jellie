# 배경 프롬프트 — 4장씩 (1400×700 고정, 총 21시트)

## 쓰는 방법
- 시트 하나 = 메시지 하나. 아래 **시트 1 ~ 21** 블록을 하나씩 통째로 복사해서 보내세요.
- 출력이 항상 1400×700이라 한 시트에 **4칸(2×2)** 으로 했어요. 칸 하나가 약 692×342라서 예전(450px)보다 1.5배 커요.
- 칸 사이는 16px 흰 간격이라 제가 안쪽을 깎지 않고 정확히 잘라요. 잘린 칸은 320×160 픽셀 격자에 맞춰 정리해서 3배(960×480)로 키워 넣어요.
- 같은 장소의 시간대·날씨 버전은 같은 시트나 이어지는 시트에 있어요. 이어지는 시트는 **같은 대화**에서 보내면 방 구조가 똑같이 나와요.
- 시트 이미지를 그대로 주시면 돼요. 파일 이름은 필요 없고, 맨 아래 표대로 제가 이름을 붙여요.
- 결과가 이상하면 같은 대화에서 한 줄로 고쳐 달라고 하세요:
  - 사람이 나왔을 때: "Remove all people from every tile. Keep everything else the same."
  - 글씨가 나왔을 때: "Remove all labels and writing. Signs show only wavy lines."
  - 색이 너무 쨍할 때: "Make the colors more muted and add a faint warm yellow film tint."
  - 가운데 아래에 가구가 있을 때: "In every tile, move furniture out of the bottom-middle; leave that floor empty."

---

## 시트 1
```
One single image, EXACTLY 1400×700 pixels (2:1). A 2×2 grid of 4 equal tiles separated by solid white gaps exactly 16 pixels wide, with a 16-pixel white margin around the grid; each tile is about 692×342 (2:1). Fill the 4 tiles in reading order (left to right, top to bottom). Every tile must be completely filled edge to edge (nothing cut off or faded at tile borders). No labels, no captions, no titles, no numbers.

STYLE (every tile): retro pixel art like a 320×160-pixel SNES / Game Boy Advance game screen scaled up, with big chunky square pixels clearly visible and aligned to one grid (no blur, no anti-aliasing). COLOR: rich, soft, emotional color with lots of smooth stepped gradients (dithered skies, gentle light bloom, color-graded shadows in blue-violet and highlights in warm gold). Muted and slightly desaturated, NOT pop and NOT neon: as if a faint warm yellow film filter were laid over the whole image, like a golden-hour nostalgic memory. About 48 harmonious colors. LIGHT: make rich, varied use of light in every tile: beams of light coming through windows or between trees, glowing halos and soft light bloom around lamps and the sun, light spilling across the floor and ground, gentle rim light on edges. SKY / WINDOW COLOR: use a wide variety of beautiful sky colors that fit the time and weather (peach, rose, lavender, teal, amber, indigo, pale mint, misty gray-blue), as smooth multi-band dithered gradients, never a flat single color. Cozy like Stardew Valley.

EVERY TILE: an empty place with absolutely no people, no figures and no animals. Eye-level, straight-on view. The bottom-middle of each tile is open, empty floor/ground right in front of the viewer; furniture, trees and props stand along the left and right sides or further back. Keep the important objects inside the central 85% of the width. All signs and screens show only wavy lines, never letters.

TILES:
1. A cozy one-room apartment living room by day: sofa on the left, TV stand on the right, window with sheer curtains in the back wall, plants, open wooden floor in the middle front.
2. The same living room in early morning: golden sunrise beams through the window across the floor, a coffee mug on a side table.
3. The same living room in the evening: warm lamps, orange-purple dusk in the window, TV glowing softly.
4. The same living room at night: blue moonlight from the window, one small warm lamp, a phone charging.
```
