# 배경 4차 — 남은 전부 (22장 = 시트 A 20장 + 시트 B 2장)

이걸로 게임에 나오는 모든 장소·시간·날씨 버전이 다 채워져요.
**시트마다 새 메시지로** 보내 주세요 (작업 중에 보내면 파일이 저장 안 돼요).

## 시트 A (20장, 4×5)

```
Create ONE image: a sprite sheet of 20 background tiles in a 5-column × 4-row grid.

GLOBAL RULES (apply to every tile):
- Style: the same cozy, cute pixel-art style as my previous sheets (Kairosoft / Stardew-like life sim). Soft, slightly muted pastel palette, clean dark outlines, gentle shading.
- Every tile is EXACTLY the same size, landscape 16:9. Separate tiles with a thin 4px pure-white gap. No borders, no labels, no numbers.
- ABSOLUTELY NO PEOPLE, no silhouettes, no animals. Empty places only (the game adds characters later).
- Eye-level, straight-on view (like a stage); slight top-down is OK. Keep the lower 40% of each tile as open floor/ground with nothing tall in the center, so two characters can stand side by side in the middle.
- No readable writing anywhere (signs may have abstract squiggles only).

TILES — fill the 5×4 grid in reading order: tiles 1–5 = first row, 6–10 = second row, 11–15 = third row, 16–20 = fourth row.

(Tiles 1–4 are the SAME cozy one-room apartment living room — sofa on one side, TV stand, rug, window in the back wall, plants — at different times/weather.)
1. Home in the morning: soft yellow sunrise light through the window, long light beams on the floor, a coffee mug on the table, sheer curtains.
2. Home in the evening: warm lamp light, orange-purple dusk sky in the window, the TV glowing softly, a blanket on the sofa.
3. Home on a rainy day: gray light, rain streaks and droplets on the window, a small lamp on, a mug of tea, cozy and quiet.
4. Home in winter: snow falling outside the window, a small heater glowing, a knitted blanket, a mandarin bowl on the low table, a tiny Christmas tree in the corner.

5. City street at sunset: the same kind of neighborhood street as before (shops on both sides, crosswalk, road into the distance), pink-orange sky, street lamps just turning on, long shadows.
6. City street in snow: snow piled on roofs and sidewalks, snow falling, warm shop lights, white road with tire tracks, gray-blue sky.
7. Café on a rainy day: inside a cozy café — counter with espresso machine in the back, wooden tables, plants — big front window streaked with rain, dim warm pendant lights.
8. Café in snow: the same café interior with snow falling outside the big window, warm lights, steaming mugs on the counter, a small wreath on the wall.

9. Café window seat: close view of a window-side bar table with two stools, a book and a latte on the table, a big window showing a quiet street, a potted plant, soft daylight.
10. Café outdoor terrace in summer: a sunny sidewalk terrace with a striped parasol, small round tables and chairs, flower pots, iced drinks on a table, bright blue sky and green trees.
11. Gym in the evening: the same gym (treadmills by the window, weight rack, benches, mirror wall), orange dusk light through the windows, lights partly on.
12. Office at night: an empty office (rows of desks with monitors, office chairs, filing cabinets), ceiling lights mostly off, a few desk lamps and monitors glowing, city lights at night through the windows.

13. University campus at sunset: red-brick campus buildings with a clock tower, a wide stone path, trees, pink-orange sky, lamp posts lit.
14. Small Korean snack restaurant (bunsik / diner): a tiny cozy eatery with a counter and a pot of tteokbokki and fish-cake skewers, a few small tables with stools, a wall menu (abstract squiggles only), a drink fridge, warm lighting.
15. Amusement park at night: the amusement-park entrance with a Ferris wheel and roller coaster behind, everything lit with colorful lights, dark blue sky with stars, ticket booths glowing.
16. Beach at night: dark navy sky with a full moon and stars, moonlight path on calm waves, sand in the foreground, a couple of folded beach umbrellas, a distant lighthouse light.

17. Beach on a cloudy day: gray overcast sky, muted sea with small whitecaps, sand, empty lounge chairs, a few seagulls far away (no animals up close).
18. Beach in winter: a cold, quiet winter beach, pale blue-gray sky, a little snow on the sand, a wooden fence, frosted dune grass.
19. Paris street in the rain: the same charming Paris street (cream Haussmann buildings, iron balconies, café awning), rain falling, wet shiny cobblestones with reflections, lamp posts lit.
20. Overseas branch office: a modern glass-walled office in a foreign city — open-plan desks, a big window with skyscrapers outside, a reception counter with an abstract company logo (no text), potted plants, bright daylight.
```

## 참고
