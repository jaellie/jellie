# 배경 프롬프트 — 1장씩 (총 83장)

## 쓰는 방법
- 장면마다 **아래 공통 프롬프트의 `SCENE:`만 바꿔서** 한 장씩 생성하세요. 그리드·시트 아님.
- 같은 장소의 시간대·날씨 버전은 같은 대화에서 이어서 만들면 방 구조가 똑같이 나와요.
- 파일 이름(예: `park_spring`)과 함께 보내 주시면 960×540으로 줄여서 넣을게요.

## 공통 프롬프트
```
One single image, 16:9 landscape, 1792×1024. NOT a grid, NO borders, NO white lines, NO frame, NO labels or text.

STYLE: retro pixel art like a 320×180 GBA/SNES game screen scaled up exactly 5.6×, with big chunky square pixels clearly visible and perfectly aligned to one grid (no blur, no anti-aliasing, no sub-pixel details).
COLOR: rich, soft, emotional color with lots of smooth stepped gradients (dithered skies, gentle light bloom, color-graded shadows in blue-violet and highlights in warm gold). Muted and slightly desaturated, NOT pop and NOT neon: as if a faint warm yellow film filter were laid over the whole image, like a golden-hour nostalgic memory. About 48 harmonious colors.

COMPOSITION: an empty place with absolutely no people, no figures, no animals. Eye-level, straight-on view. The bottom-middle third of the image is open, empty ground/floor right in front of the viewer (a character will stand there). Furniture, trees and props sit along the left and right sides or further back. Fill the whole canvas edge to edge, nothing cropped at the corners. Signs and screens show only wavy lines, never letters.

SCENE: [장면 설명]
```
