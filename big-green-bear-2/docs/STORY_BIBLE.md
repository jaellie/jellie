# Story Bible: Big Green Bear's Adventure II, THE OTHER BEAR

Single source of truth for plot facts. Code, evidence text, dialogue and art must follow it.
**LOCKED** = decided by the author. **PROPOSED** = drafted by Claude to make the design work, needs the author's OK. **OPEN** = not decided.

---

## 0. The point of the story
The twist "Green did it" is not the main point. The main point is **what BGB (the bear the player controls) knew after the death and chose to hide.**
The player believes they directed BGB the whole game, but never saw his most important choice. The ending should feel like betrayal by someone the player trusted.
Final question: **"How far is a person who knew the truth and stayed silent responsible?"**

## 2. The time, 11:47 vs 11:52
### 2.1 Mechanism (LOCKED principle, PROPOSED details)
- At 11:47 the tower loses power and the clock stops.
- **Recommended and used here: the clock movement is electric, so it stops with the blackout.** Nobody tampered with it. This keeps the mystery about the twins, not about machinery.
- The blackout cause is a **separate fact that must be proven separately**. PROPOSED: the tower's old main fuse trips at 11:47 (Clockkeeper's maintenance log shows it had tripped before). It is not tied to the killer.
- Rule: the game never needs a player to solve a clock trick.

### 2.2 Timeline (fixed)
| Time | Event | Who knows |
|---|---|---|
| ~11:40 | Edward tells BGB: "Tonight, don't trust anyone's face." | BGB, player |
| 11:47 | Blackout. Clock stops. A cry is heard. | everyone in the tower |
| 11:47 to 11:52 | Green and Edward face each other over the records. They keep talking in the dark. | Green only (and Edward) |
| 11:52 | Scuffle. Edward falls and dies. **His pocket watch stops at 11:52** from the impact. | Green |
| right after 11:52 | **BGB meets Green and learns Green is involved.** | BGB, Green |
| later (OPEN: exact time) | Help is called late. BGB gives a delayed, incomplete statement. | see section 5 |
| later | Edward's body is found. | everyone |

### 2.3 How the player reaches 11:52 (fair clues)
No single clue proves it. The player must combine:
1. Damage on Edward's pocket watch (stopped at 11:52).
2. A **sound witness** (a thud or a voice heard around 11:52). PROPOSED: Night guard or Café owner.
3. **Facility records** (power log shows the blackout at 11:47, not later).
Wrong inference: "the stopped clock is the time of death." The game lets players believe that first.

## 3. Who is the real Big Green Bear
### 3.1 Three layers of names
| Layer | Left-handed child | Right-handed child with the ear scar |
|---|---|---|
| Name at birth | **Green** | **Big Green Bear** |
| Name in official records after the flood | Big Green Bear (swapped) | Green (swapped) |
| Name the village uses now | **Big Green Bear** (what the player controls) | **Green** |

So **the bear the player has controlled all game was born as Green**, and the bear the village calls Green was born as Big Green Bear.

### 3.2 Body clues (must never change)
Game convention is the side seen on screen. Anatomical wording from the author is shown in brackets.
| Trait | Current "BGB" | Current "Green" |
|---|---|---|
| Handedness | uses the hand on the image-RIGHT in front view (his own left hand: **left-handed**) | image-LEFT (his own right hand: right-handed) |
| Scarf tail | image-RIGHT chest (his own left side) | image-LEFT chest (his own right side) |
| Ear scar | none | the ear on the **image-LEFT** (his own right ear), old healed scar |
| Smile | eyes closed | eyes slightly open |
This matches the existing sprites: Green = the same sprite mirrored, with the scar overlay.
- **Note on "left ear":** I read the author's "left ear scar" as the ear on the **screen-left**, as in `CANON_DECISIONS.md`. If it should be Green's own left ear instead, the overlay moves to the other ear (a one-line change). **Please confirm.**

### 3.3 How the player discovers the swap
Compare **old photos** with the **flood records**:
- Pre-flood photos show the **left-handed** child with the name "Green" on a tag or on his belongings.
- The flood records list the scarred child as "Green".
- PROPOSED: the **jade bell** is engraved with a small "G" and originally belonged to the child born as Green (the player's bear). It ties the bell evidence to the identity question.
- PROPOSED: the scar was **acquired in the flood**, so pre-flood photos show no scar and the handedness is the only physical identifier. That is why the records could be wrong.

## 4. The three responsibilities (never merge them)
| # | What | Who | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The fall:** the scuffle that led to Edward's death | **Green** | LOCKED |
| 2 | **Delay:** BGB met Green, knew he was involved, and delayed calling for help and giving his statement | **BGB** | LOCKED |
| 3 | **Concealment:** both brothers stayed silent about the identity record and the death | **BGB and Green** | LOCKED |

- **Cause of death: fatal injury from the fall.** The game never claims that BGB's delay caused the death.
- Whether faster help could have changed the outcome is **never settled by the game** (PROPOSED, deliberately unknowable). BGB's delay is a **separate moral responsibility**, not a causal fact.
- The motive is C: Green did not plan this. Fear of losing his name met an old wound (PROPOSED example: Edward's records show Green's name was taken in the flood paperwork, and nobody corrected it).

## 5. What BGB knew and hid (the core mechanic)
This is the part to design most carefully. Everything below is **LOCKED as intent, PROPOSED as detail**.

### 5.1 Concealment ledger
| What BGB knew | When he learned it | What he did | Evidence the player can find |
|---|---|---|---|
| Green was involved in the fall | right after 11:52 | delayed help, stayed silent | gap in his timeline; late call record |
| The identity records were swapped | before the story (OPEN: since when) | kept quiet | his earlier statements; Edward's note |
| He was the one born as "Green" | OPEN: when he learned this | never told the player | see ending |
| Where he was between 11:52 and the call | immediately | gave a vague statement | his first and second statements differ |

### 5.2 Player experience rule
- The player controls BGB, but **BGB's statements are written by the game**, not chosen by the player.
- **There is a hole in BGB's own timeline** (his minutes after 11:52). In the case file this appears as a blank entry or a vague line, never as a clean lie.
- Interrogations that touch BGB's whereabouts show evasive but plausible answers. They must read as innocent on the first playthrough and as lies on the second.
- **Ending:** the missing minutes are shown. The player sees BGB with Green, and sees him choose. That is the betrayal moment.
- The player never gets to make that choice. They only discover they never could.

### 5.3 Fairness rule
Every hidden fact must have at least one clue the player could have noticed. No fact may be created only at the ending.
